Replace Driving Licence

⚠
No Licence on Record

You do not hold a driving licence that can be replaced. You must pass your theory and practical tests first.

ℹ
Licence Already in Possession

Your driving licence is already in your inventory. A replacement is only issued if your licence is lost or stolen.

You can apply for a replacement if your driving licence has been lost, stolen or destroyed. A replacement licence will be issued with your existing categories and entitlements intact.
⚠
Replacement Fee: £20

Replacement licences are ordered on lsgov.co.uk (Driving and transport → Driving licence and tests). You pick a Postal Prime locker and collect it there. Your old licence stops working as soon as you order.
