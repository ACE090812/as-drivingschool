MOT Test

All vehicles over 3 years old must hold a valid MOT certificate under the Road Traffic Act 1988.

⚠

Driving without a valid MOT may result in a fixed penalty notice and 3 penalty points.

Drive your vehicle to an approved MOT testing centre. A qualified mechanic will inspect your vehicle and issue a pass or fail certificate. Cost: £55.
